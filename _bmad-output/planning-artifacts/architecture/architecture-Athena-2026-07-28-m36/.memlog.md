- (constraint) M35/M36 supersede the stale M34 annotated-SVG rule: SVG may expose only data-athena-ref geometry references; Athena source owns all semantic and representation metadata.
- (decision) AD-1: M36 uses a compiler-pipeline architecture with authority-gated planner adapters; Athena source is SSOT and renderer/frontend are derived consumers.
- (decision) AD-2: Connectable Entity Contract is a narrow compiler view over existing component knowledge and project entities; it cannot redefine product identity or become a second component catalog.
- (decision) AD-3: Engineering Lowering emits snapshot-bound transient Connection IR and Layout Graph; neither is persisted source truth or independently editable.
- (decision) AD-4: Lowered constraints carry owner and strength: semantic, representation, physical, or layout preference; planners may trade preferences only.
- (decision) AD-5: Port-to-Anchor binding is explicit and fail-closed; data-athena-ref identifies geometry only and cannot infer a Port, role, direction, or compatibility.
- (decision) AD-6: The Kotlin compiler owns planner invocation, normalization, and validation. M36 Core uses Athena-native deterministic planning; ELK is a replaceable optional JVM adapter.
- (decision) AD-7: Route Intent is distinct from Connection and Route Fact; accepted route evidence records endpoints, anchors, occurrences, channels, planner, snapshot, quality, and source provenance.
- (decision) AD-8: Cabinet physical policy remains the gate for mounting, clearance, duct/channel feasibility, and route validity; generic planner output cannot override it.
- (decision) AD-9: LSP and renderer consume normalized typed facts only; renderer is paint-only and all user edits route through governed intent, source mutation, compile, and rerender.
- (decision) AD-10: M36 acceptance combines structured semantic route proof with source-first Cabinet E2E; screenshots alone never establish correctness.
