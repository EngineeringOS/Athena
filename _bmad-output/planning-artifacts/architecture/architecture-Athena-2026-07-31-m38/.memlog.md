- (decision) Use a fail-closed compiler trust chain paradigm: one typed attachment authority flows from Port binding through occurrence transform, route IR, presentation connector, and paint.
- (decision) RepresentationDefinition retains identity and package metadata but contains one IntrinsicRepresentationContract; remove PresentationAnatomy authority, body-authority variants, compatibility shells, and explicit representation fallback behavior.
- (decision) Representation Anchors remain geometry-only; TerminalPortBinding owns semantic Port to Anchor identity with a stable binding id, and duplicate terminal/port fields are removed from Anchor geometry.
- (decision) Replace partial EndpointAttachmentFact models with one complete immutable attachment carrying connection, role, Port, binding, Element, occurrence, Anchor, geometry ref, source/local/resolved coordinates, transform identity, snapshot, and provenance; FALLBACK is not a valid kind.
- (decision) GraphicOccurrence is the sole post-placement geometry contract; its transformed Anchors and body share one immutable transform, and all terminal markers, hit geometry, and route endpoints consume the exact resolved attachment coordinate.
- (decision) OrthogonalRouteIr is validation and normalization only in M38: replace endpoints from attachments, remove zero-length segments, merge collinear segments, validate alternation and explicit junction/crossing/line class; never search for a better route.
- (decision) There is one RouteFact-to-PresentationConnector lowering path; PresentationConnector carries required source/target attachment evidence and validated route points, with no nullable endpoint ids, zero-coordinate defaults, center fallback, or reconstruction in LSP/Theia.
- (decision) SVG renderer and Theia are paint/inspection consumers only; missing trusted geometry is a blocking compiler/LSP diagnostic and no renderer or workbench helper may synthesize coordinates or endpoint identity.
- (decision) M38 is a hard replacement: delete legacy/compatibility/fallback models, duplicate lowerers, stale tests, milestone comments, generated proof assumptions, and obsolete docs in affected paths; no deprecated aliases, adapters, defaults, or migration code.
- (decision) M38 acceptance uses a dedicated examples/m38/professional-control-drawing project, structured equality/trust assertions, LSP inspection evidence, desktop/narrow Electron screenshots, source-set hygiene, stale-authority audit, and sequential full verification.
- (decision) M39 owns Projection Composition Graph and placement intelligence; M40 owns route planning and optimization. M38 exposes trustworthy contracts only and adds no planner, ELK, layout, route, or label language.
- (decision) No new kernel module: representation-model owns IntrinsicRepresentationContract and TerminalPortBinding; presentation-model owns GraphicOccurrence, resolved endpoint evidence, OrthogonalRouteIr, and PresentationConnector; compiler owns all cross-model lowering and validation; routing-model retains authored/planned RouteFact only.
