# PRD Quality Review - Athena M43 Presentation and Rendering Reality

## Overall verdict

Approve for architecture and story breakdown. The PRD fixes presentation authority, keeps the
four-divisible scalar grid contract explicit, and defines live product evidence. Remaining physical
unit and batching choices are correctly deferred to implementation rather than hidden in authoring
syntax.

## Decision-readiness - strong

The primary outcome, hybrid SVG/Canvas boundary, colocated companion authority, and no-compatibility
policy are explicit. The `cell: K` rule removes an avoidable authoring ambiguity.

## Substance over theater - strong

Requirements are tied to a real rolling-shutter product surface and source trace workflow. Pattern,
AI, export, and EPLAN compatibility are excluded instead of inflating the milestone.

## Strategic coherence - strong

All features serve one thesis: validated engineering meaning becomes an inspectable live document.
Counter-metrics prevent syntax growth, renderer inference, and compatibility shims.

## Done-ness clarity - adequate

FR consequences are testable. Exact browser performance thresholds and physical unit mapping remain
implementation details and are called out as open questions.

## Scope honesty - strong

Non-goals and out-of-scope sections explicitly fence Pattern, export, AI, import, procurement, and
legacy paths.

## Downstream usability - strong

Glossary terms, FR/NFR/SM identifiers, user journeys, and architecture references are stable for
epic/story creation.

## Shape fit - strong

This is a brownfield, multi-surface developer product; named journeys, source-set hygiene, runtime
revision consistency, and product screenshots are appropriate.
