# M38 Review Rubric

M38 is ready only when every answer is yes.

## Product

- Can an engineer describe normal source without layout, route, paint, or renderer mechanics?
- Does every visible line attach to the exact intended terminal?
- Can human and AI trace that endpoint to Athena source?
- Does dedicated M38 example prove this in Theia?

## Architecture

- Is `RepresentationDefinition` sole intrinsic geometry/Anchor authority?
- Is `GraphicOccurrence` sole placed geometry/Anchor authority?
- Is strict `PresentationConnector` sole visible connection authority?
- Are route candidates compiler-internal and untrusted?
- Does Theia paint without endpoint, topology, crossing, label, or style recovery?
- Are M39 placement and M40 routing intelligence still deferred?

## Cleanup

- Are fallback, compatibility, duplicate lowering, legacy anatomy, and stale production names deleted?
- Are old tests/docs rewritten or removed instead of preserved around shims?
- Do source-set hygiene and encoding audits pass?

## Proof

- Do structural tests prove exact endpoint equality and complete trace?
- Do fresh desktop and narrow screenshots exist?
- Do sequential kernel, LSP, frontend, and Electron checks pass?
