# PRD Quality Review - M42 Engineering Knowledge System

## Overall Verdict

PRD is decision-ready for architecture after three bounded corrections. Strategic thesis, authority
boundaries, failure states, breaking scope, and closure proof are unusually explicit. Remaining risk
is downstream interpretation of public JSON openness, Glossary shorthand, and legacy-loader scope.

## Decision-Readiness - Strong

The PRD states decisions as commitments: Knowledge System is cross-cutting, Engineering Reality owns
subjects, Knowledge and Validation contracts are separate, compatibility is forbidden, and M43 owns
rendering. Rejected work is visible in Sections 11.2, 15, and counter-metrics.

## Substance Over Theater - Strong

UJ-1 directly exercises the milestone thesis. NFRs are specific to determinism, fail-closed
engineering authority, open contracts, bounded execution, and cross-domain integrity. No persona,
innovation, or generic scalability furniture appears.

## Strategic Coherence - Strong

All five Feature groups serve one arc: establish typed project authority, compile governed
definitions, evaluate canonical Reality, expose evidence, and falsify the result through one product
fixture. SM-2 directly measures Engineering Intelligence Density while counter-metrics prevent
ontology, autonomy, drawing, and compatibility drift.

## Done-Ness Clarity - Adequate

Every FR contains testable consequences. Failure modes distinguish `INCOMPLETE` from `INVALID`, and
closure requires product-surface agreement plus legacy absence.

### Findings

- **Medium - JSON openness is underdefined (FR-21, NFR-4).** JSON Schema shape and canonical byte
  rules are separated, but required fields and unknown-field policy are not explicit. *Fix:* require
  deliberate `required` and additional-property policy in both schemas, plus a named canonicalization
  contract.
- **Medium - Legacy loader wording can over-delete (FR-29).** "knowledge loader" can include the new
  package-local Athena Source compiler. *Fix:* say "retired legacy knowledge loaders" and audit by
  named superseded contract inventory.

## Scope Honesty - Strong

Non-users, Out of Scope, Risks, counter-metrics, and migration policy expose omissions. No unresolved
product assumption or phase-blocking question remains. Architecture mechanisms are correctly moved
to `addendum.md`.

## Downstream Usability - Adequate

FR, UJ, and SM IDs are contiguous and unique. Domain terms are defined before Feature use.

### Findings

- **Medium - Glossary contract names and prose shorthand drift (Section 4 onward).** Entries define
  `EngineeringEntity`, `EngineeringFunction`, and related code-level names while prose frequently
  uses Entity, Function, and Relationship. *Fix:* make human terms canonical Glossary entries and
  place public contract identifiers in parentheses.

## Shape Fit - Strong

Capability-first structure fits a chain-top brownfield platform PRD. One named journey proves user
value without turning a kernel milestone into UX theater. Brownfield Delta, API policy, standards
evidence, risks, and closure gates are warranted by launch-grade stakes.

## Mechanical Notes

- FR IDs are contiguous from FR-1 through FR-29.
- Only UJ-1 is defined and every UJ reference resolves.
- SM IDs are unique: SM-1 through SM-8 and SM-C1 through SM-C4.
- No placeholder, stale Engineering Knowledge Reality term, `[ASSUMPTION]`, or `[NOTE FOR PM]`
  remains.

## Resolution

All findings resolved in `prd.md`:

- JSON Schemas now require declared dialect/identity, required fields, and deliberate unknown-field
  policy; canonicalization is a separate published specification.
- Legacy deletion now targets a named superseded-contract inventory and says legacy loader.
- Knowledge Package governance now has an exact M42 boundary.
- A failed well-formed blocking Constraint is explicitly `INCOMPLETE`; malformed knowledge remains
  `INVALID`.
- UJ-2 and UJ-3 cover Knowledge Package maintainer and tool integrator workflows.
- Success metrics now explicitly cover every FR.
- Glossary now makes human terms canonical and maps public contract identifiers parenthetically.
